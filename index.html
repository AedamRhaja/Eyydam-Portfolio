Smartpoint Glasses

Smart Assistive Eyeglasses System for Individuals with Severe Mobility Impairments

Smartpoint Glasses operates as the cursor or mouse for individuals who cannot use their hands or arms due to illness, motor disabilities, or paralysis, but whose fingers remain functional. Natural head tilts guide fluid on-screen cursor movement, while accessible low-effort finger switches on a companion module handle clicking. Features dwell selection, MAX30102 pulse oximetry, emergency SMS alerts, offline voice commands, and CareMotion ESP32-C3 Wi-Fi synchronization.

Focus areas

Head-Tilt Cursor Finger Controls Assistive Tech ESP32-C3 MAX30102 Sensor
Assistive Problem & Core Purpose: Designed specifically as the cursor or mouse for individuals who cannot use their hands or arms due to illness, motor disabilities, stroke, or paralysis, but whose fingers are functional. Head tilts control on-screen pointer movement fluidly, while low-actuation finger buttons handle clicks effortlessly without requiring arm mobility.

Key System Capabilities & Engineering Scope:

  • Head-Tilt Cursor Navigation: Continuous wearable motion sensing on the eyeglass frame measures yaw and pitch angles, translating intuitive head movements into smooth, real-time on-screen cursor control.
  • Tactile Finger Clicking: Dedicated, low-actuation tactile buttons on the compact companion unit allow functional fingers to trigger left click, right click, and drag functions with minimal physical effort.
  • Dwell-Based Clicking: Configurable dwell-time selection allows completely hands-free target confirmation when finger resting is needed.
  • Vital Signs Monitoring: Integrated MAX30102 PPG pulse oximeter sensor continuously streams real-time heart rate (BPM) and blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) right from the user's finger.
  • Emergency Alert & Caregiver SMS: Physical panic switch on the finger box and automated biometric threshold triggers dispatch urgent SMS alerts with synchronized BPM telemetry to designated caregivers.
  • Offline Voice Command Recognition: Edge-processed voice recognition recognizes essential assistive commands completely offline, protecting privacy and functioning without internet access.
  • Smart Reminder System: Programmable auditory and visual reminders for medication routines, hydration, and positional shifts.
  • CareMotion IoT Ecosystem: Synchronized over Wi-Fi (http://10.179.36.86) to the CareMotion companion mobile application, showing live connection status (CMT-C3-001), telemetry logs, and caregiver controls.

Power Supply

Design & Implementation of Regulated 220V AC to 12V DC System

Focused on the hardware design, electrical engineering, and hands-on physical implementation of a benchtop power supply converting 220V AC mains electricity into clean, regulated DC voltage up to 12V DC. The project involved mastering the complete power conversion process, selecting and calculating appropriate components (step-down transformer, diode bridge rectifier, capacitive smoothing filters, and heatsinked linear regulator), and ensuring safe, stable voltage delivery for electronic circuits and embedded systems.

Focus areas

220V AC to 12V DC Voltage Regulation Power Conversion Hardware Design Embedded Systems
Hardware Engineering & Conversion Scope: Hands-on electrical design, circuit assembly, and hardware implementation converting 220V AC mains electricity into clean, regulated DC voltage up to 12V DC. The system provides stable, protected power output with multi-level voltage adjustment (3V, 4.5V, 6V, 9V, 12V) for electronic prototyping, sensor testbeds, and embedded microcontroller projects.

Key Conversion Stages & Electrical Architecture:

  • Step-Down Transformation: Heavy-duty iron-core transformer steps down hazardous 220V AC mains utility voltage to safe, low-voltage AC levels while providing galvanic isolation.
  • Full-Wave Bridge Rectification: Rectifier diode network converts alternating bidirectional AC sinusoidal waveforms into pulsating unidirectional DC current.
  • Capacitive Filter Smoothing: High-value reservoir electrolytic capacitor (2200µF / 35V) filters AC ripple voltage, delivering a continuous smoothed DC bus with minimal ripple.
  • Linear Voltage Regulation & Step Selection: Active linear regulator circuit with thermal heatsinking maintains a tight, ripple-free voltage reference. Features a multi-position selector offering calibrated stepped output rails: 3V, 4.5V, 6V, 9V, and 12V DC.
  • Benchtop Safety & Thermal Dissipation: Incorporates primary fuse protection, chassis ground isolation, an extruded aluminum finned heatsink for thermal safety under continuous loads, a front panel power status LED, and dual positive/negative banana output binding posts.
  • Safe Power for Embedded Loads: Delivers clean, transient-free DC power to prevent brownouts, logic resets, and noise in sensitive electronics, sensors, and microcontrollers.
